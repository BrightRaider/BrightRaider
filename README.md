## Why BrightRaider?

Dark corners hide enemies. NVIDIA's game filters are blocked in many games with anti-cheat, the monitor menu is slow, and alt-tabbing to change settings gets you killed. BrightRaider puts brightness, colours and the FPS limit on one key — per game, switched by itself when the game comes to the front.

> **VibranceGUI is no longer needed.** BrightRaider switches vibrance and the FPS limit per game as you alt-tab — everything VibranceGUI does, in one place.

## What it does

### Free — for every game

- **Brightness on a key** — Normal / Bright / Brighter, instant, even in fullscreen.
- **Every game its own** — colours and FPS limit per game, on while the game is in front, back to normal on Alt-Tab. Replaces VibranceGUI.
- **G-SYNC / FreeSync setup** — V-SYNC, low latency and the right FPS limit in the driver, the [Blur Busters](https://blurbusters.com/gsync/gsync101-input-lag-tests-and-settings/14/) way, with an exact undo.
- **HDR per game** and **dim your other screens** while you play.
- **No Windows key in games**, the mouse kept on the game's screen, your microphone on one key.
- A **setup** that walks you through, a **guide** beside every settings page, every key rebindable.
- NVIDIA, AMD and Intel · multi-monitor · one portable EXE under 6 MB, nothing to install.

## FAQ

**Does it work with my game?** Yes — BrightRaider changes your display, not the game. Any PC game, any launcher.

**Will I get banned?** Brightness, colours, FPS limit and the overlays work next to the game — nothing injected, nothing read, no game file changed. The module tools that press keys for you are a different kind; each one says so on its page. Details: [Anti-cheat safety](docs/Safety.md).

**Do I need Pro?** No. Free covers what most players need: profiles on a key, colours and FPS limit per game, the G-SYNC / FreeSync setup. Pro adds sound, crosshair, Auto-Brightness, Autorun and the Arc Raiders tools.

**I play with a controller.** The display side needs no key at all: profiles, colours, FPS limit and HDR switch by themselves when the game comes to the front. For the keys, let Steam Input, DS4Windows or reWASD send a keyboard key from a controller button — more in the [Manual](docs/Manual.md).

**The overlay doesn't show.** Crosshair, mute icons and the Map Scanner need the game in **borderless** — Windows draws nothing over exclusive fullscreen.

More: [FAQ on the website](https://brightraider.github.io/BrightRaider/#faq) · [Known issues](https://github.com/BrightRaider/BrightRaider/issues/74)

## System requirements

- Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit — one EXE, nothing to install, no runtime.
- NVIDIA or AMD for everything (vibrance, hue, FPS limit); Intel and integrated graphics get brightness and contrast.

## Uninstall

1. *Settings → App & License* → turn off **Start with Windows**, then right-click the tray icon → **Exit**.
2. Delete `BrightRaider.exe`.
3. Delete the folders `%APPDATA%\BrightRaider` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BrightRaider` (modules live there).
4. Optional: remove `GdiIcmGammaRange` under `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion\ICM` — the full brightness range BrightRaider unlocked.
